Now Hiring Lab & Research

Research Assistant – FMT Manufacturing, Testing, and Analysis

Manipal, Karnataka Full-Time 2 Positions

Detail-oriented lab professional to support stool sample processing, FMT capsule preparation, and quality control testing for clinical studies. Work spans DNA extraction, qPCR, CFU counts, microbial plating, and strict GMP-like documentation.

Full Job Description

Role Summary

We are looking for a detail-oriented Research Assistant to support the processing of stool samples, FMT capsule preparation, and quality control testing for clinical studies. The role involves DNA extraction, qPCR, CFU counts, microbial plating, and strict documentation under biosafety and GMP-like conditions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Handle, label, and process human stool samples for FMT.
  • Assist in FMT capsule formulation, encapsulation, and storage.
  • Perform microbial CFU counts and sterility testing.
  • Extract DNA and run qPCR assays.
  • Maintain detailed records, SOPs, and QC documentation.

Requirements

  • Master's in Microbiology, Biotechnology, Life Sciences, or a related field.
  • Minimum 1 year of experience in human sample processing, DNA extraction, and PCR/qPCR.
  • Experience in microbial culturing and plating.
  • Proven ability to work under biosafety protocols and SOPs.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Knowledge of GMP/FMT protocols and microbial metagenomics.
  • Precision and consistency in lab procedures and documentation.
  • Strong troubleshooting, organizational, and time management skills.
  • Ability to independently execute SOPs and maintain strict quality standards.
  • Excellent record-keeping and communication skills.

Now Hiring Computational Biology

Senior Computational Biologist

Bengaluru or Manipal Full-Time 1 Position

Lead the computational backbone of Jivanu's platform — WGMS bioinformatics pipelines, AI-driven recommendation engine, India-relevant reference cohort, and computational design of next-generation microbial therapeutics. Build and lead the computational biology team.

Full Job Description

About the Role

We are looking for a Senior Computational Biologist to lead the computational backbone of Jivanu's platform. You will lead the development of our computational analytics and bioinformatics pipelines, creating innovative methodologies that power a robust, clinically deployable platform for microbiome multi-omics analysis.

As the platform grows, you will have the opportunity to build and lead a computational biology team while working closely with the founders on scientific and product strategy.

Key Computational Platforms

  • WGMS Profiling Platform. End-to-end design, implementation, and clinical-grade scaling of deep whole-genome metagenomic sequencing pipelines — QC, assembly, taxonomic and functional profiling, longitudinal tracking, and clinical reporting.
  • AI Recommendation Engine. Lead continued development of the engine that translates microbiome profiles into personalized diet, prebiotic, probiotic, supplement, and therapeutic recommendations, including disease-specific modules and clinical history integration.
  • India Reference Cohort. Lead the construction, curation, and scientific use of an India-relevant microbiome reference cohort — a foundational asset and globally distinctive dataset that does not exist today.
  • Therapeutics-Side Computation. Drive computational design and selection of next-generation probiotic strains, donor-independent microbial consortia, and engineered live biotherapeutics — comparative genomics, metabolic modeling, ecological dynamics, and target-trait optimization.
  • Engineering Rigor. Set the standard for reproducibility, version control, containerization, workflow orchestration, and data infrastructure as the platform scales to clinical and regulated environments.
  • Team & Scientific Direction. Hire, mentor, and lead the computational biology team; partner with the founders on scientific roadmap, publications, IP, and external collaborations.

Required Qualifications

  • PhD with several years of postdoctoral, faculty, or industry research experience in microbiome, metagenomics, or microbial genomics.
  • Direct experience in WGMS, 16S sequencing, strain-level analysis, functional profiling, and longitudinal modeling.
  • Strong experience building scalable bioinformatics pipelines using Python and/or R, Unix/Linux, Nextflow/Snakemake, Docker/Singularity, and cloud platforms (AWS or GCP).
  • Practical expertise applying statistical modeling and machine learning to high-dimensional biological data.
  • Ability to scope problems independently, prioritize work, and communicate clearly to clinicians, wet-lab scientists, and non-specialists.

Preferred Qualifications

  • Returning to India — or considering a return — after experience at top-tier global research institutions or biotech/pharma R&D groups.
  • Accomplished computational biologist in India waiting for a scientifically ambitious, founder-led platform.
  • Demonstrated depth through publications, software, deployed systems, or significant industry contributions.
  • Experience with engineered microbes, microbial consortia design, or genome-scale metabolic modeling.
  • Familiarity with regulated clinical/diagnostic environments (CLIA, CAP, CDSCO, FDA, or equivalent).
  • Demonstrated ability to mentor scientists and build high-performing computational teams.

How to Apply

Please send a CV and a cover letter describing your prior relevant experience, why this role interests you, and links to any published work, software, or open-source contributions you are proud of. References will be requested later in the process.

Send CV + cover letter + links to published work to hr@jivanutx.com

Program Manager – Donor Program

Full Job Description

About the Role

We are looking for a dynamic and driven Program Manager to spearhead our Donor Program — a first-of-its-kind initiative to identify rare individuals with exceptionally healthy microbiome profiles. This is a high-impact, highly visible role at the intersection of science, community outreach, and strategy.

You will work closely with senior leadership and our R&D team to design, execute, and scale the program across India, engaging with sports academies, military training institutes, healthcare organizations, and government regulatory agencies.

Key Responsibilities

  • Identify and recruit target donors; develop strategic participation plans across multiple locations in India.
  • Own end-to-end execution of the Donor Program from planning through delivery, driving cross-functional collaboration.
  • Build and manage relationships with partner institutions, NGOs, and agencies that support the program's goals.
  • Gather and analyze donor insights to continuously refine outreach strategies, improve conversion rates, and generate high-quality leads.
  • Design and run targeted campaigns in donor-rich areas to drive awareness and participation.
  • Present program updates, strategies, and budget proposals to internal stakeholders and senior leadership.

Required Skills

  • Bachelor's degree in engineering, science, marketing, business, or a related field.
  • Fluency in English, Hindi, and Kannada; proficiency in Malayalam and other Indian languages strongly preferred.
  • Proven track record of building and executing programs in volunteering, community engagement, or donor management.
  • Strong interpersonal and people engagement skills — ability to build trust quickly.
  • Established networks with social organizations and NGOs.
  • Willingness to travel across India.
  • Exceptional attention to detail, organizational skills, and ability to manage multiple priorities.

Preferred Qualifications

  • MSW, MBA, or postgraduate degree.
  • Proficiency in multiple Indian languages; extra weightage for Kannada and Malayalam.

What We Offer
